Scope: whole Electron app (index.html shell, css, all js/screens). Mode: Operate.
Audience: NSWMA ops clerks parsing WhatsApp reports, and managers reading status. Task: parse, review, produce weekly sheet, read performance.

## Direction contract

THESIS: WCIS is the supervisor's carbonless duplicate book. White leaf is the original record, canary is the review copy, pink is the file copy. It refuses the navy-sidebar KPI-card admin template.

OWN-WORLD: Jamaican-green bookcloth cover holds a stepped index-tab rail; the leaf is bright white with green pre-printed form ruling, carbon-blue entered figures, rubber-stamp statuses (COLLECTED green, MISSED red, PENDING carbon blue, LATE ochre, BACKLOG plum), Jamaican gold as highlighter for selection and the active week. Archivo across widths (condensed form labels, expanded black zone numerals), Courier Prime only for raw typed report text. Sharp 2px corners, perforation lines, plies peeking under the leaf.

STORY: A clerk sees which step of the book they are on, fills it, tears to the next tab. A manager opens the book to the roster of 26 zone plates and reads the stamped tallies for the period.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Dashboard leaf: stub header with title and period ruler (week segments, gold highlighter on the selected range) across the top; tally row of stamped counts; zone plate roster dominant below; activity and alerts as a narrow carbon column.
